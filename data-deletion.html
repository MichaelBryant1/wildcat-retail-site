Disconnect access

Independent Publications Ops does not currently provide a public consumer login. If Meta shows it as connected to your account or business, remove it through Meta's settings:

  1. Open settingsIn Facebook or Instagram, open Settings and privacy, then Settings.
  2. Find integrationsOpen Business integrations or Apps and websites. The wording may vary by account and device.
  3. Remove accessSelect Independent Publications Ops, review the removal choices, and confirm.
Immediate effectRemoving an integration stops future access granted through that connection. It does not automatically erase material already stored by Meta.

Request deletion

Email support@wildcatretail.com with the subject Independent Publications Ops data deletion request.

Include only the minimum information needed to locate the record, such as the Facebook or Instagram profile or Page name involved and a brief description of the interaction. Do not send passwords, access tokens, financial information, or government identification.

What happens next

Wildcat Retail will review the request to identify data reasonably linked to the requester. Proportionate verification may be requested when needed to prevent deletion of another person's information.

Matching personal information will be deleted or de-identified unless it must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legal claims. If no matching personal information is held, the response will say so. Because there is no public consumer login, most readers will have no Independent Publications Ops account record.

What this does not erase

  • Posts, comments, reactions, messages, or other content held by Meta. Use Meta's controls for those records.
  • De-identified aggregate insights that no longer identify an individual.
  • Minimal audit or suppression records required for security, duplicate prevention, legal compliance, or honoring an unsubscribe request.

For more information, read the privacy policy.
